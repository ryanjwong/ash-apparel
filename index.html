Our Story

Los Angeles Since 2020

Established in 2020, ASH Apparel began with a vision to support the local Los Angeles apparel community with better menswear blanks. In just a few years, we've grown to support a network of over 100 independent screen printers and distributors who rely on us for the "best bang for their buck."

We understand the hustle of the apparel industry. That's why we focus on delivering garments that are not only premium in feel but also priced to keep your business competitive. At ASH, you're a partner. We thrive on personal relationships and work one-on-one with our customers to ensure our blanks meet their exact specifications.
