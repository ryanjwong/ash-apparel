Our Story

Los Angeles since 2020

FAQ

Wholesale blank apparel questions

The things buyers ask us most.

Do you sell wholesale blank t-shirts?

Yes. We supply screen printers, apparel brands, merch companies, distributors and bulk apparel buyers.

What is the garment dyed / vintage wash tee?

Style 1112 is a 200 GSM (about 6oz), 100% cotton heavyweight tee that is garment dyed and vintage washed for a soft, faded, broken-in look. Colors: Charcoal, DL Blue, Cream, Navy, Blue Jean and Black.

Are your blanks good for screen printing?

Yes. They are made for screen printing, DTG, DTF, embroidery, branded merch and private label programs.

Do you offer bulk blank hoodies?

Yes. The 280 GSM heavyweight fleece hoodie (Style 3090) is available in bulk at wholesale pricing.

What are your minimums?

We sell by the box, and each box is one size and one color: 72 pieces for tees and long sleeves, 48 for the Style 1112 garment dyed tee, and 24 for fleece hoodies.

Where are you located?

Los Angeles, California. We serve buyers, printers, brands and distributors locally and ship nationwide.
